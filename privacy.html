Dogfathers Plus ("Dogfathers Plus," "we," "us," or "our") provides a premium mobile dog-grooming service and booking application (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices you have. By creating an account or using the Service, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

We collect information you provide and information generated as you use the Service:

  • Account information: your name, email address, and phone number.
  • Service address & location: the street address, city, and ZIP code where grooming takes place, and approximate geographic coordinates derived from that address to match you with a nearby groomer.
  • Pet information: your pet's name, breed, weight, age, coat type, photos, and grooming or medical notes you choose to share.
  • Appointment & loyalty data: bookings, service history, before/after grooming photos, loyalty points, and reward redemptions.
  • Communications: messages you send to staff or groomers through the Service.
  • Technical data: device and browser information, and data stored locally on your device (such as login session and preferences) to operate the app.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate you.
  • To schedule, assign, deliver, and manage grooming appointments.
  • To match appointments with the nearest available groomer using location data.
  • To operate the loyalty program (earning and redeeming points).
  • To send service-related communications (booking confirmations, reminders, password resets, and account notices).
  • To provide customer support and improve the Service.
  • To detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, and security issues.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to run the Service:

  • Groomers & staff: assigned groomers receive the appointment details necessary to perform the service (your name, service address, pet details, and relevant notes).
  • Service providers: vendors that host and power our app, including our database and authentication provider (Supabase), our address-to-coordinates (geocoding) provider, and our email provider. These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms.
  • Legal & safety: when required by law, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Dogfathers Plus, our customers, or others.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

5. Cookies & local storage

The Service uses your browser's local storage to keep you signed in and remember preferences (such as theme and "remember me"). We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

7. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards, including encrypted connections and database row-level access controls, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your privacy rights (including California / CCPA)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect about you and how we use it.
  • Request access to or a copy of your personal information.
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal information.
  • Opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information — note that we do not sell or share your information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising your privacy rights.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below. We will verify your request before acting on it.

9. Children's privacy

The Service is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
